compared per subject: the official syllabus units, the headings actually
present in the notes, and the topics the tracker lets you mark as covered.

A unit can be **covered but untracked** (notes exist, tracker cannot
certify it) or **tracked but unsupported** (tracker will happily accept a
state change for material with no notes behind it). Both are reported.

## How to read this

"covered + tracked" means a syllabus unit has note headings AND a
tracker topic, so marking it covered corresponds to real material.
Everything else is a gap that a passing build and a green test suite
will not tell you about.
